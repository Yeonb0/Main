- Tight bound : 상한과 하한이 같은 범위

> [!note] Formal Definition
> ![[Pasted image 20260629092222.png]]
> ![[Pasted image 20260629092229.png]]
> - $g(n)$ 은 $f(n)$ 의 점근적 tight bound


> [!note] Theorem
> 
> $$
>	f(n) = \Theta(g(n)) \ \text{if and only if} \ f = O(g(n)) \ \text{and} \ f = \Omega(g(n))
> $$
